The mojo-syntax skill documents latest Mojo; on 1.1.0 stable: `match`, `while case` and `if case` are parse errors ('unexpected token in expression') - use if/elif and `while opt:` + `.value()`. You can't move a field out of a struct that has a synthesized destructor ('destroyed out of the middle of a value') - give it a `deinit self` accessor (see `Next.take_state`). Traits used for generic struct fields need `Deinitable` (`trait Utils(Deinitable, Movable)`). `@fieldwise_init` conflicts with an explicit `__init__`. t-strings are `TString`, so wrap in `String(t"...")` for String params. An unannotated list literal of structs infers to `Array[T, N]`, not `List` - annotate. `Python.dict(**{...})` doesn't compile; use a `PythonObject` dict literal.
